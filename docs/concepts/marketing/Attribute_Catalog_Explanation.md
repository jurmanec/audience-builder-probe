# Attribute Catalog: Concept and Use in Telecom Marketing

## Concept of an Attribute Catalog

An **attribute catalog** (or attribute catalogue) is a governed, centralized inventory (or “dictionary”) of attributes—the named, typed properties that describe customers, households, products, or services—along with their definitions, allowed values, data types, sources, ownership, privacy classifications, and usage rules.

It acts as a single source of truth so that the same meaning is applied consistently across CRM, customer data platforms (CDPs), product catalogs, advertising systems, billing, and analytics. Related concepts appear in marketing platforms (SAP CRM “attribute sets” and “attribute lists” used for segmentation; Experian-style marketing attributes; Salesforce Data Cloud/Data 360 attributes for segment building) and in product information management (PIM) systems that organize product characteristics into families, groups, and templates.

### Core Elements

- **Attributes**: Individual descriptors such as “household income bracket,” “preferred viewing genre,” “current internet speed,” “churn risk score,” “data usage percentile,” or “bundle eligibility.”
- **Organization**: Grouped into sets, categories, or families (e.g., demographic, behavioral, product technical, marketing).
- **Governance metadata**: Definitions, value domains, update frequency, lineage, consent/privacy flags, and who can use them.
- **Purpose**: Enable reliable filtering, segmentation, personalization, configuration, and measurement without inconsistent or duplicated fields.

## Use by a Telecom Firm

A large telecom operator manages complex offerings (internet tiers, TV packages, mobile, equipment, bundles) and rich household-level data from tens of millions of subscribers. An attribute catalog supports marketing in two tightly linked ways: customer/household targeting and product/offer configuration.

### 1. Customer and Household Attribute Catalog for Targeting and Personalization

Marketing and advertising teams draw on first-party deterministic data from subscriber households (viewing behavior across linear TV, VOD, and streaming; broadband usage; equipment; billing and tenure) plus third-party demographic, psychographic, and purchase insights.

Typical attributes in the catalog include:

- Demographics and household composition
- Viewing trends and content affinities
- Purchase behaviors and intent signals
- Geographic and location attributes (down to ZIP or PMA level)
- Service tenure, usage patterns, device ownership, and modeled propensities (upgrade likelihood, churn risk, interest in mobile or premium packages)

These feed:

- Addressable TV and multiscreen campaigns (different ads to different households watching the same content)
- Audience segments built from a large library of targetable attributes
- Lookalike modeling, propensity scoring, and AI-driven audience discovery
- Personalized offers on websites, apps, call centers, email/SMS, and direct channels
- Measurement and attribution (linking exposure to outcomes such as site visits, sales, or brand lift)

### 2. Product and Service Attribute Catalog for Offers and Recommendations

A parallel (or integrated) catalog defines what can be sold and how it can be configured:

- Internet: download/upload speeds, data caps vs. unlimited, latency
- TV/video: channel lineups, premium add-ons, DVR/4K features
- Mobile: data allowances, 5G access, device financing
- Equipment and bundles: modem/gateway types, compatibility rules, pricing components, eligibility constraints (e.g., speed availability by area)

Marketing uses this to match customer attributes to product attributes for upsell/cross-sell, dynamic bundles, promotions, and configure-price-quote (CPQ) processes. Modular attributes reduce time-to-market for new offers and keep messaging consistent across channels.

## Practical Marketing Outcomes

- Higher relevance and conversion rates through precise targeting and personalized recommendations
- Improved retention and ARPU via timely, attribute-driven offers
- Stronger advertising products, because the operator can sell targeted inventory to brands using these audiences
- Consistent data quality, privacy compliance, and reduced silos across systems
- Faster campaign and product launches because teams reuse governed attributes instead of creating ad-hoc fields

In short, the attribute catalog is the structured foundation that turns raw customer and product data into reliable, reusable building blocks for segmentation, personalization, offer management, and measurement. Those capabilities matter in a data-rich telecom environment.
